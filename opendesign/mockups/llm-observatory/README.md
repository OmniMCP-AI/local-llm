
### Profile guidance

Catalog profile HTML, Markdown and typed release records use the reviewed
`profile-guidance-v1` editorial templates in `catalog-guidance.mjs`. Each
published strength quotes a source-backed catalog field and links its source;
limitations identify missing local fit or performance evidence. The templates
are reviewed rules, not unreviewed generated suggestions. Change the version
when their wording or selection rules change. Guidance is descriptive and does
not establish compatibility or measured speed.

A profile without a qualifying fact says its strength evidence is unavailable.
Those profiles still need source review before they can meet the one-to-five
strengths coverage goal; a missing fact is not a negative assessment.
