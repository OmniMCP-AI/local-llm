An evidence record joins a claim to its source, scope, and revision. This prototype uses illustrative fixtures so you can review how that relationship is presented.

## Check the basis

- Review the artifact, runtime, context, and setup before treating two figures as comparable.
- A community summary without raw trial conditions can inform a decision without qualifying for a controlled ranking.
- Fit estimates answer a capacity question and may require a hardware check for speed or reliability.

## Follow the chain

- Go from a chart mark to an evidence record and from the record to the owning machine or model.
- Read the [benchmark method](#wiki/benchmark-method) for exclusion rules.
- Review [agent response examples](#agents) for evidence IDs and missing-result behavior.
