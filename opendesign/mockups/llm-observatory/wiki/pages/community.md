Open Feedback on any page to send a private note. The hosted site gives you a receipt and automated guidance. The local prototype keeps the note on this device.

## Include what lets somebody reproduce it

- A page, model artifact, hardware build, runtime version, and exact workload are often more useful than a general suggestion.
- [Request a benchmark on GitHub](https://github.com/OmniMCP-AI/local-llm/issues/new?template=benchmark-request.yml) or [report a result correction on GitHub](https://github.com/OmniMCP-AI/local-llm/issues/new?template=result-correction.yml). Enter only the details you want to share publicly. Keep private notes and receipt links out of the issue.
- Read [what counts as evidence](#wiki/evidence-guide) or [browse the source catalog](#catalog) before filing a missing listing.
