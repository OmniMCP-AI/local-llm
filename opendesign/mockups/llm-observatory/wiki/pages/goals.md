Start with two requirements. Add a constraint only when it could change your decision. Use one metric to order the remaining matches.

## Reading the results

- Unknown values do not satisfy a limit. A comparison can miss one goal and have incomplete evidence on another.
- A tradeoff candidate cannot be improved across all selected measured metrics at once within this comparison.
- Single-user output and prompt processing, four-user response time, and wall energy are separate measurements. See the [benchmark method](#wiki/benchmark-method).

For exact hardware and model sources, use the [source catalog](#catalog). To request an observation, open [community feedback](#wiki/community).
