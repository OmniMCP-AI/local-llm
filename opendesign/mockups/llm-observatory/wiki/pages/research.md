The use-case filter describes the subject of a publication. Open its test records for the source-reported task, hardware, model, runtime and conditions. Task and measurement filters apply together to individual records.

A method article can explain a benchmark without contributing local test records. Missing dates and unreported tasks stay visible. Use the [benchmark method](#wiki/benchmark-method) and the [evidence guide](#wiki/evidence-guide) to interpret a result.
