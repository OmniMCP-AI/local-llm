## Extraction and labels

- Use one article-topic subject per document. Cite each supported article topic where it appears. Keep the title and short summary in the sampled source language. A product feature, methodology section or deployment option supports an article topic at its cited scope.
- For droid-llm, take both timing cells from every row of the real-device comparison table. The caption identifies them as chat-session screenshots. Use the Simplified Chinese section (`zh-Hans`, with no region inferred) and exclude the separately labeled placeholder export table. Preserve the author’s hardware and cross-model comparability caveats. Individual prompt lengths, run counts and settings are unknown.
- For Splitwise, take the TTFT and energy entries for both workload columns of Table IV, the two phase power findings in Figure 8 and the optimized transfer timings from Figure 14. Keep the paired A100 and H100 values together for each Table IV metric and workload. Take the throughput and provisioned-power headlines in the first two scenarios of Section VI-C and the memory-use hypothesis for later conversation requests in Section VII. The resulting cases cover measured timings, a measured electrical phase comparison, a modeled cluster and a qualitative forecast.
- Assign a case task only when the cited exercise identifies it. Tag real handset chat captures as interactive chat and the Table IV replays by the source trace workload (coding or conversation). Table IV reports request performance using replayed lengths. The application prompts and output quality are unknown. Tag the trace-driven cluster capacity cases as multi-user serving. Leave phase characterization, isolated transfer and the speculative memory discussion unclassified for task.
- Use the quantity itself as the measured dimension. Electrical power or energy stays electrical when a token-processing phase qualifies it. First-token latency, decoding rate and isolated transfer latency are distinct dimensions. Use projection for modeled or hypothetical outcomes even when they describe quantities that can also be measured. Preserve acronym and phase wording from exhibits where practical. Evidence and conditions describe the source’s method and caveats.
- Score source-level topics and case-level tasks and dimensions against the frozen annotations. Use the evaluator’s definitions of precision, recall, abstention, false article-to-case transfer and projection classified as a measurement. Keep all predictions and errors as calibration evidence. Neither report can authorize promotion or publication.
